# Town of Dartmouth | Civic Research

Independent, nonpartisan, evidence-based research into how Dartmouth, Massachusetts, operates, makes decisions, and uses public resources.

**Purpose:** Make local government easier to understand and identify practical opportunities for transparency, improved services, and cost savings. This is an independent civic project, not an official Town of Dartmouth website.

## What we're building

- **Financial transparency:** approved budgets, actual expenditures, revenue, debt, reserves, enterprise funds, and vendor payments.
- **Government map:** boards, departments, responsibilities, official contacts, and decision processes.
- **Meeting intelligence:** searchable decisions, votes, commitments, and source-linked timelines.
- **Efficiency opportunities:** documented problems, potential remedies, costs, possible savings, and follow-up.
- **Records request log:** requests, responses, production dates, and missing documents.

## Current priorities (November 2026)

1. Obtain and normalize the financial transaction data requested November 10, 2026.
2. Inventory existing official budget, financial, and meeting sources.
3. Reconstruct multi-year financial trends from authoritative sources.
4. Identify 1–3 specific, testable service or process improvements.
5. Publish only substantiated, source-linked findings.

## Research principles

1. Original records over summaries; preserve source URLs and retrieval dates.
2. Distinguish **verified fact**, **unverified claim**, **analysis**, and **proposed action**.
3. Never infer misconduct solely from a payment, relationship, or unusual number.
4. Provide appropriate context, alternative explanations, and a chance to respond before publishing allegations about identifiable people or organizations.
5. Never publish protected personal details, banking identifiers, or unrelated private information.
6. Record corrections and changes; do not silently rewrite a finding.

See [research standards](research/RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md) and [official source register](research/OFFICIAL_SOURCES.md).
