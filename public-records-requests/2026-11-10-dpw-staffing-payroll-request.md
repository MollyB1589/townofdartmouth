## Requested records

1. Current DPW organizational chart and employee roster with divisions, positions, job titles, and employment status.
2. Authorized, filled, and vacant positions and full-time equivalents (FTEs) by division.
3. Existing payroll and compensation records, including annual base pay, overtime, and total gross compensation for FY2022–FY2026.
4. Historical headcounts/FTEs by division and latest available FY2027 staffing information.

Scope includes all DPW divisions, including water, sewer, solid waste, and other enterprise-funded units. Requested existing records in electronic, preferably CSV/Excel, format; asked for redaction of exempt data and itemized fee estimate before costs.
