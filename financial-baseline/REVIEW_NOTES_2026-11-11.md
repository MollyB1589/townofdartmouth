## Critical research findings to confirm next

1. **Audits and disclosures.** Verify the claimed FY2024/2025 finding inconsistency and actual filing/submission deadlines using FY2024 and FY2025 original audit packages and the Federal Audit Clearinghouse. FAC *acceptance* is not necessarily the *submission* date. Check any COVID-era deadline extensions before labeling a particular year's submission late. Audit packages were not accessible through the current PDF browsing path for this review; no conclusion has been reached.
2. **Enterprise funds.** Verify audited reserves, operating results and the indirect cost methodology for water/sewer/solid waste. Avoid treating depreciation as cash flow; distinguish user-fee revenue, unrestricted net position and retained earnings.
3. **Staffing & productivity.** The already-sent DPW staffing request is the main blocker. Reconcile authorized vs. filled FTE by division, enterprise-funded employees, overtime, contracting, and measurable work volume; compare with true peers by road miles and service obligations.
4. **Peer comparison.** Reproduce per-resident calculations and peer-selection method. Fire districts are separate; compare tax and service burden consistently. Lower costs could mean efficiency *or* different service levels.
5. **Capital plan.** Confirm project scope, prices, school reimbursement assumptions, and dates. The apparent $516.5 million in the report is a proposed long-range borrowing scenario, **not approved spending**.
