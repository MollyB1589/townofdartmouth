## Data intake standard

For each new document or dataset, record:

- **Title / identifier**
- **Source agency and direct URL**
- **Document's covered period** (not just upload date)
- **Retrieved date and publication date, if available**
- **Original file type / dataset structure**
- **Public access status and reuse restrictions**
- **Notes on exclusions and limitations**
- **Verification status**

Do not count a missing document on a website as proof that the Town does not hold the document. Use a records request where appropriate.
